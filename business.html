1 Project 1: The impact of part time employment on students academic performance.

    • Teamwork
    • Problem Solving and Critical Thinking

Strenghthened my teamwork, problem solving and presentation skills while gaining a better understanding of how technology can address challenges within a university environment.

2 Project 2: Business Intelligence Dashboard - UK Carbon Emissions Analysis

Project Overview: developed an interactive dashboard as part of my second year business intelligence module to support finance and sustainability stakeholders in making informed budgeting decisions for energy-efficient, low-carbon homes.

  • Problem Identified: stakeholders needed a clear understanding on three key questions:
    • What is UK's national carbon emissions situation?
    • How does the UK's compare with other European countries?
    • Which fuel type contributes most to the UK's carbon emissions?
  • Proposed Solution: developed an interactive and easy to read dashboard using Microsoft Power BI.
    • Used line chart to compare the uks carbon emisions per capita with those of other european countries, highlighitng differences over time period from 1990-2022.
    • Used clustered bar chart to compare carbon emissions across different fuel types, identifying which fuel contributed the most to the UK's overall carbon emissions.
    • Used Power BI card visual to display total UK CO2 emissions, CO2 emissions per capita and CO2 emissions per unit of GDP.
  • My Role and Contribution: analysed the available carbon emissions data and selected appropriate charts to answer each question. Designed and organised the dashboard to present information clearly.
  • Skills and Approaches Adopted:
    • Data Analysis
    • Data Visualisation
    • Microsoft Power BI
    • Problem Solving and Critical Thinking
    • Business Intelligence
  • Outcome and Learning: successfully developed a dashboard improving my practical experience using Power BI. Strenghthened my ability to transform raw data into meangingful visual insights that support stakeholder decision-making.

3 Project 3: Heather Breakfast and Lunch Restaurant - Digital Transformation.

Project Overview: developed a user-friendly website that helps the restaurant improve customer engagement, increase operational efficiency and provide a better customer experience.

  • Problem Identified: Heather faces several issues due to relying on outdated, paper-based methods. Table bookings require customers to phone during opening hours, causing inconvenience. Paper booking forms and feedback cards create waste and take staff time to organise and process. Manually entering feedback makes it difficult to identify customer preferences and improvements. Additionally, the lack of a website and social media limits Heather’s ability to promote the restaurant, advertise offers and new menu items, and allow customers to view the menu online.
  • Approach and Solution: I propose developing a user-friendly website. The website will include table booking request form, digital feedback form and social media links. The website will be designed to be visually appealing, easy to navigate and mobile-friendly.
  • Skills and Approaches Adopted:
    • Web App Development
    • Problem Solving
    • Coding
    • Github
  • Outcome and Learning: The proposed website aims to reduce paperwork, save staff time and make booking requests more convenient for customers. Digital feedback will make it easier to organise customer opinions and analyse them to support better business decisions. Social media links, online menus and promotional offers will also help improve the restaurant's online visibility and attract potential customers. Through this project, I have developed a better understanding of how web technologies can solve real-world business problems. I have also learned how digital transformation can improve business efficiency, customer engagement and decision-making. Additionally, the project has helped me understand the importance of designing a user-friendly and mobile-responsive website that meets both business and customer needs.
