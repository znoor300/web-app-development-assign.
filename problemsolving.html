Business Context

This case study focuses on a restaurant that wants to improve its digital presence and modernise its customer services through digital transformation. The restaurant aims to provide a more convenient and accessible experience for customers using a website.

The main business problem is that customers may find it difficult to access restaurant information, make booking enquiries and communicate with the business. Traditional telephone-based enquiries can also create additional work for restaurant staff.

The proposed solution is to develop a responsive website using HTML, CSS and JavaScript. The website will provide online booking requests, customer feedback, restaurant contact information and social media links. These features will improve communication between the restaurant and its customers.

The overall goal is to improve customer satisfaction, strengthen the restaurant's online presence and support more efficient business operations. This project also demonstrates how web development can be applied to solve real-world business problems without requiring an expensive database system.

Privacy, Security & GDPR

Customer privacy and data protection are important considerations when developing a restaurant website. The proposed solution considers UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Privacy

Only necessary customer information, such as name, email and booking requirements, should be collected. Customers should be provided with a clear privacy notice explaining how their information is used.

Security

The website should use HTTPS, input validation and secure hosting. Customer information should be protected from unauthorised access and should not be stored unnecessarily.

GDPR & Data Protection

Customers should be informed about their data protection rights, including access, correction and deletion. Information should only be processed for an appropriate lawful purpose.
