Restaurant Digital Transformation Case Study

Business Context

This case study focuses on a restaurant that wants to improve its digital presence and modernise its services through digital transformation. The main aim is to create a professional website that provides customers with easy access to restaurant information and online services.

One of the main challenges faced by the restaurant is that customers may have difficulty making booking enquiries, accessing contact information and providing feedback. Depending only on traditional communication methods can also increase the workload of restaurant employees.

The proposed solution is to develop a responsive website using HTML, CSS and JavaScript. The website will include restaurant contact details, social media integration and an interactive booking and feedback form. JavaScript will automatically generate an email using the information entered by customers.

The overall goal is to improve customer experience, increase online visibility and support better communication between the restaurant and its customers. This digital solution demonstrates how web development can be applied to solve a real-world business problem.

Privacy, Security & GDPR

Customer privacy and data protection are important when developing a restaurant website. This project considers the requirements of UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Privacy

Only necessary customer details, such as name, email address and booking requirements, should be collected. Customers should receive clear information about how their personal data is used.

Security

The website should use HTTPS, input validation and secure hosting to protect customer information. Personal information should not be stored or shared unnecessarily.

GDPR

Customers should be informed about their data protection rights, including accessing, correcting and requesting deletion of their personal information.
