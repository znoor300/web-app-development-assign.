HEATHER

Restaurant Digital Transformation Case Study

Business Context

Heather is a fictional breakfast and lunch restaurant that wants to improve its online presence and make its daily operations easier through digital technology.

Currently, Heather uses paper-based methods to manage table bookings and collect customer feedback. Customers have to call the restaurant during opening hours to book a table, which can be difficult for people with busy schedules. The restaurant also uses paper forms to record feedback from customers. This leads to unnecessary paper waste and makes it difficult and time-consuming to organise and analyse the information. Staff have to manually enter feedback into a computer before they can analyse it and understand what improvements are needed.

Another problem is that Heather does not have a website or social media presence. This makes it harder for the restaurant to promote special offers, advertise new menu items and communicate with potential customers. Customers also cannot easily view the restaurant's menu online before deciding to visit.

To solve these problems, as Chief Technology Officer I have created a user-friendly website that works on both computers and mobile phones. The website includes a table booking request form, a separate digital feedback form and social media links. It will also generate an email containing the customer's details, which can be sent directly to the restaurant without needing a live database. Overall, the website aims to reduce paperwork, save staff time, make communication easier, attract customers and help the restaurant make better business decisions using customer feedback.

Privacy & GDPR Compliance

Customer privacy and data protection are important when developing a restaurant website. Heather only collects personal information that is necessary to manage customer enquiries, booking requests and feedback. Information entered into the website forms, such as names, email addresses and booking details, will be used to respond to customers and manage their requests. Customers should avoid entering sensitive personal information into the forms. As this is a demonstration website, booking information is not stored in a database. Instead, booking requests generate a pre-filled email that customers can send directly to the restaurant. Customer information should be handled securely and only kept for as long as necessary. Customers can contact the restaurant if they wish to ask about their personal information or request its correction or deletion, where applicable.

Cookie reminder: This demonstration website does not use non-essential tracking cookies. If analytics or advertising cookies are introduced, appropriate consent must be obtained before they are activated.
