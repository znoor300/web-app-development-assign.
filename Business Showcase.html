Problem-Solving Page Development Rationale

This section explains why I designed the Problem-Solving Page in the way I did.

1. Clear Structure

I used clearly separated sections so that users can understand each stage of the problem-solving process. The structure follows a logical progression from identifying a problem through to evaluating the result.

2. User Experience

The page was designed with the user in mind. Headings, paragraphs, spacing and visual sections help users quickly identify important information without being overwhelmed by large blocks of text.

3. Visual Consistency

Consistent typography, colours, spacing and card layouts were used throughout the page. This creates a professional appearance and makes the content easier to navigate.

4. Responsive Design

The page was designed to work on desktop, tablet and mobile devices. CSS media queries allow the layout to change depending on the user's screen size.

5. Accessibility

Semantic HTML elements such as header, section, article and footer were used to give the webpage a meaningful structure. Clear headings also help users understand the content hierarchy.

6. Easy to Evaluate

The project was organised around the problem, role, solution, outcome and skills. This makes it easier for an assessor to see what I contributed and what I learned from the project.

My Problem-Solving Approach

I used a structured approach when developing my projects.

1

Identify

Understand the problem, requirements and needs of the intended users.

2

Research the problem and consider different possible approaches and solutions.

3

Develop

Create the solution, test ideas and make improvements based on feedback.

4

Evaluate

Review the outcome and identify what worked well and what could be improved.

Reflection and Learning

Completing these projects helped me develop both technical and transferable skills. I improved my understanding of HTML and CSS while also learning how important planning and organisation are when developing a website.

The Problem-Solving Page was particularly important because it allowed me to apply a structured approach to presenting a problem and its solution. I learned that good web design is not only about appearance; it is also about making information clear, accessible and easy to understand.

In future projects, I would like to improve my use of JavaScript and interactive features while continuing to focus on usability, accessibility and responsive design.
